AKSU CHARGING STATIONInvestment Overview · October 2026

Power Where Students Live and Study.

A phone and laptop charging station inside Akwa Ibom State University — a daily-need service, not a trend.

The Opportunity

The Problem

Students on campus struggle daily to keep phones and laptops charged. A dead phone means no calls home, no class materials, no connections — and queues at the few available sockets waste hours.

The Solution

A dedicated charging caravan stationed on campus: fast phone and laptop charging at a small fee, plus power-bank rentals so students charge on the move. Power is a need students pay for every single day.

The Offer

Every naira is a proportional share of monthly profit

  • Double-your-money option: you receive your share of the station's monthly net profit until you have received 2× your contribution. At expected utilization, a ₦100,000 supporter is repaid in about 10 months and finishes doubling in about 20.
  • Permanent stake option: keep your share as ongoing equity and receive profit distributions for as long as the station operates.
  • Full transparency: every contribution is recorded in a shared ledger with the date, amount, and contributor. Supporters receive a monthly statement of revenue, costs, and payouts. The school land agreement is shared with supporters before funds are collected.
