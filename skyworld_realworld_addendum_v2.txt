SKY WORLD — REAL WORLD LAYER v2 (replaces earlier addendum)
Paste into Gemini AFTER the master prompt.
Informed by a code study of the real game "Akwa Ibom Life".
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

REFERENCE GAME: "Akwa Ibom Life" is a 3D life simulation. Players walk
around real places (Ibom Plaza, a stadium, a market, universities) in
third person, on a REAL clock, with real-geography travel and live
players. Sky World is that, for EVERY place on Earth.

HOW TO GET "REAL, EVERYWHERE" WITHOUT HAND-MODELLING THE WORLD:

1. REAL MAP: MapLibre GL JS (or Leaflet) with free OpenStreetMap tiles.
   Any city, town or village on Earth is selectable via the free
   Nominatim geocoder. Player spawns where they search.

2. REAL VENUES: query the OpenStreetMap Overpass API around the player
   for amenity=school/university/library/marketplace/bank/place_of_worship/
   restaurant/bar/nightclub/hospital/gym/stadium/park. Show each with its
   REAL NAME and real distance. Cap ~40 nearest, lazy-load while moving.

3. PROCEDURAL 3D (the key): from OSM building footprints in view, extrude
   simple 3D buildings with Three.js (height from OSM tags or a default).
   Roads as ribbons. Landmark venues get a small signature model from a
   template library by type (school, church, mosque, market, stadium,
   hospital, bank). Third-person character walks the real street layout.
   Mobile-first: low-poly, aim 30fps on a mid Android phone.

4. DATA-DRIVEN CONTENT: all gameplay reads tables ("new content is new
   rows"): SKILLS, TRAITS, DREAMS, BIRTH LOTTERY, NEEDS (hunger, energy,
   fun, social, hygiene, bladder), SHOP, FURNITURE+ACTIONS (pose, minutes,
   effects), JOBS, SCHOOLS. Locale packs add currency, language flavor,
   foods, slang and festivals per country; a fallback pack covers any town.

5. REAL CLOCK: the game runs on the player's real local time for the
   chosen place (use the timezone of that location). Activities are quick
   real countdowns, not hidden instant stat bumps.

6. BIRTH LOTTERY: weighted starts (wealthy, middle-class, struggling,
   village-born) with different money, starting skills and perks, fitted
   to the local economy of the chosen country.

7. LIVING WORLD, LATER PHASE: nearby players, chat, visiting houses,
   card games. Phase 1 is single-player/offline; multiplayer needs a
   backend and comes after the single-player world feels alive.

BUILD ORDER: (a) place search + real map + walking character;
(b) real named venues from Overpass; (c) extruded 3D buildings;
(d) full needs/skills/jobs/school engine from data tables;
(e) birth lottery + locale packs; (f) polish; (g) multiplayer.
Start with (a) and (b). Confirm you understand the difference between
a drawn world and a REAL map-driven world before you begin.
