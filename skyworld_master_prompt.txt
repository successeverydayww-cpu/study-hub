SKY WORLD — MASTER BUILD PROMPT
(Paste this entire prompt into Gemini / Google AI Studio)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Build me a browser-based, mobile-first, offline-capable PWA life
simulation game called SKY WORLD.

THE VISION: It must play and feel exactly like the Nigerian viral game
"Lagos Life" — but GLOBAL. The player can live in ANY real place on
Earth: any country, any region/state, any city, town or local
government. A player in Berlin gets Berlin; a player in Uyo gets Uyo;
a player in Tokyo gets Tokyo. Same engine, local soul.

═════════════════════════════════════════════
PART 1 — THE ENGINE (non-negotiable mechanics)
═════════════════════════════════════════════

1. CHARACTER CREATION: name, avatar, brief origin story. The character
   is a visible sprite that WALKS the city map and appears inside
   every venue scene.

2. IN-GAME CLOCK: every action costs minutes; the clock visibly advances;
   sky changes Morning → Afternoon → Evening → Night; sleeping rolls
   the day. Time is compressed (1 real second ≈ 10 game minutes).

3. LIFE METERS: hunger, energy, fun, social, hygiene, health — each
   decays daily and each action restores or drains them.

4. SCENE-BASED ACTIONS (the heart of liveliness): every action plays as
   a scene — the character walks to the venue, takes a POSE (sit, eat,
   dance, sleep, work), shows a speech bubble with an emoji, for the
   action's minutes while a progress bar runs. Aim for hundreds of
   actions across dozens of venues (jobs, food spots, schools, gyms,
   religious centers, banks, markets, bars/clubs, parks, hospitals).

5. MOODLETS: 200+ named mood states, each with emoji, mood value (+/-),
   and duration in hours ("Promoted! +25 for 24h", "Broke -10 for 6h").
   They decay over time and drive an overall MOOD meter. Shown as chips
   on the character card.

6. STORY FEED: a running narration log with headline + emoji + tone
   (good/bad/info) for everything that happens — paydays, rent, gossip,
   power outages, exam results, promotions. Life must read like a story.

7. NAMED NPCs: other citizens with names, personalities and dialogue
   lines walk the streets; the player can interact/gist with them.
   NPCs also drive relationships.

8. CAREERS & EDUCATION: job listings with salary tracks and promotions;
   school enrollment, classes, exams with grades; skills that gate
   better jobs. Salaries pay weekly; rent/bills are due weekly.

9. RELATIONSHIPS: friendship → dating → marriage → children, with
   relationship meters affected by shared activities and gifts.

10. ECONOMY: local currency; bank account (savings + interest);
    investments; buy food/pantry, clothes, phones; rent apartments,
    buy land, businesses, and furnish homes room by room.

11. WORLD EVENTS: daily weather roll; country-appropriate events
    (power outages, traffic jams, market days, festivals, rain season).

12. ASPIRATIONS: long-term goals (degrees, careers, wealth, family)
    that grant big rewards and structure progression.

13. CONSEQUENCES: unpaid rent = eviction warnings; crime has police
    records; health collapses if meters stay at zero. Actions must have
    STORY consequences, not just stat bumps.

═════════════════════════════════════════════
PART 2 — THE GLOBAL LAYER (the hard part)
═════════════════════════════════════════════

Do NOT hand-write content for every city on Earth. Instead:

A. VENUE/ACTION TEMPLATES: define ~40 generic venue types (market,
   eatery, school, bank, clinic, gym, place of worship, park, club,
   barbershop, transport hub...), each with a template action list
   (bubble, pose, minutes, cost, stat effects, moodlets).

B. LOCALE PACKS: each country/city is a small data pack — currency
   name/symbol, timezone, typical weather, local food names, local
   first names, landmark venues, local slang flavor lines, local
   event names. Ship packs for at least 12 major cities across
   continents (Lagos, Abuja, Nairobi, Accra, London, New York, Berlin,
   Paris, Dubai, Mumbai, Tokyo, São Paulo) plus a FALLBACK generator
   that adapts any other city/town from its country's data
   (language, currency, region name like "Local Government Area" vs
   "County" vs "District").

C. PLACE PICKER: at character creation, choose Country → Region →
   City/Town with a search box. Every real country must be selectable.
   Small towns get the country fallback pack with their region name —
   never a "sorry, not available" answer.

═════════════════════════════════════════════
PART 3 — TECH & QUALITY BAR
═════════════════════════════════════════════

- Single HTML file or minimal files; runs fully client-side;
  localStorage saves; works on an Android phone browser; no backend,
  no accounts, no payments, nothing real-money.
- Mobile-first UI, dark theme, fast (<2s load).
- QUALITY BAR: it must FEEL alive — walking character, scene actions,
  bubble + progress, advancing clock, moodlet chips, story headlines,
  named citizens, weekly rent/salary rhythm. A plain stat menu with
  buttons FAILS the bar even if it has content.
- Build it in one pass, complete and runnable, then wait for my
  review. I will test it and give you iterative fix prompts.

Begin with the character creator + one full city (Lagos) fully alive,
then add the locale pack system and the place picker, then the rest.
